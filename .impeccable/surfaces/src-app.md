Scope: the whole FightFlow app shell and its screens (tabs, modals, timer, Shadow Cam UI). Mode: Operate.
Audience/job: amateur boxers logging and reviewing training on a phone at the gym or a laptop at home, glancing from a distance.
Out of scope: the 3D boxer figure and the tracking skeleton overlay.

## Direction contract

THESIS: FightFlow as a sports-federation design programme, after Otl Aicher's Munich '72 system: one strict grid, one grotesk, and a colour programme where every colour has a job. It refuses the category default of near-black, one hot red accent, condensed all-caps poster type and bordered cards.

OWN-WORLD: Night-blue ground (#0B1222), flat tinted panels separated by hairlines, square-ish 4px corners. Colour programme: sky (jab, primary action), green (cross), orange (hook, streak), violet (uppercut), lime (body), silver (neutral). Each tab owns one programme colour for wayfinding. Archivo throughout; numerals heavy, tabular and tight-tracked; labels in sentence case.

STORY: The fighter opens the app and reads the one number that matters on each screen at arm's length, sees their work as colour-coded rounds, logs the next session in two taps, and trusts the numbers because they look measured, not hyped.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Fighter tab on a phone: top programme band in sky; name and rank left, level numeral large right; XP bar as a segmented band; streak, sessions and rounds as a tabular numeral row; one full-width primary action "Log training"; Shadow Cam and Timer as a two-up row; the week drawn as stacked round bands. Translucent tab bar with each tab's colour bar over its active icon.

FORM: Munich '72 colour-programme world, candidate 7 of 7 on the ordered list. seed:6615f62658c15a55. Signature move: rounds drawn as stacked programme-colour bands (week chart, session rows, tier progress), plus colour-coded wayfinding per tab.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
